This entity offers enumerations for common security goals across any communication or data flow. These goals are: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, Authenticity, Reliability and Accountability.<br/>The entity is named in accordance with ISO/SAE 21434 3.1.20 and is synonymous with a security goal. To avoid confusion with the SecurityGoal entity, which is derived from the standard, the term SecurityProperty is used in deviation from the literature.<br/>

Attribute Scope Type
Confidentiality Public
Notes: Confidentiality ensures that private or confidential is not made available or disclosed to unauthorized users, and that users control (or influence) what information related to them may be collected and used, and to whom it is disclosed.<br/>
Integrity Public
Notes: Integrity ensures that information is not changed (modifies) or destroyed in an unauthorized way.<br/>
Availability Public
Notes: Availability ensures that a system works promptly, service is not denied to authorized users, and access to and use of information is timely and reliable.<br/>
Authenticity Public
Notes: Authenticity is the property of being genuine and able to be verified and trusted. Authenticityis ensured through authentication processes that verify whether users are who they say they are (entity authenticity).<br/>
Reliability Public
Notes: Reliability is the property of a system such that reliance can be justifiably placed on the service it delivers.<br/>
Accountability Public
Notes: Accountability refers to the requirements for actions of an entity to be traced uniquely to that entity (e.g., non-repuditation of a communication that took place).<br/>
