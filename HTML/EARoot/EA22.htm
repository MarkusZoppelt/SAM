Persuasion represents the persuasion principles that a Social Engineer uses to achieve his or her goals. These eight principles include Reciprocity, Obligation, Concession, Scarcity, Authority, Consistency and Commitment, Liking, and Social Proof.<br/><br/>We use the String-Type for the attributes, because we do not want to constrain the user. For default and for calculating a vulnerability score the examples in the description could be used.<br/>
